# ADR-006 Indonesia locale and fixed demo clock

## Decision

1. Ship the demo in **Bahasa Indonesia** with Indonesian regions and IDR.
2. Fix analytics “today” at **2026-09-05** so seed narratives (Sumatera August decline) stay stable.

## Consequences

- Wall-clock date changes do not break the hero demo
- Specs and docs must not refer to APAC as the decline region
